Honest bit

Things that will be harder than you expect

  • The heat at 1pm, even in December.
  • Being stared at in villages. Rarely unkind, always noticeable.
  • Nobody answering your question directly. “We'll see” is a full answer here.
  • Sounds at 5am: dogs, crows, a temple bell, a mixer.
  • How quickly you'll stop checking your phone.

Honest bit

Things that will be better than you expect

  • How safe South Goa feels, including at night.
  • How much people will want to feed you.
  • The light between 5 and 6pm.
  • A whole afternoon with nothing to do, and it being fine.
  • Cheap, excellent, unhurried food — every single day.

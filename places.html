Places

A short list on purpose. Every one of these is somewhere I'd actually take you — not a list of everything that exists in South Goa.

How I picked these

The rule for this list

Somewhere I'd go on a normal Tuesday, not just somewhere with good photos. If it's on here, it's because the food, the view or the people are worth the drive — usually all three.

There are hundreds of other places. These are the ones I'd be sad if you left without seeing.

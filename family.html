07 · Who's who

Family

Twenty-five people, five families, and a wedding that puts all of them in one room. Here's who's who before you walk in.

Two rules for the first meeting. Everyone older than you is “Aunty” or “Uncle” until they tell you otherwise — that's not a guess, that's correct. And when someone asks “did you eat?”, the answer is “yes, but I could eat again.”

Fair warning

Questions you will definitely be asked

  • “Have you eaten?” (the answer is always no)
  • “How is the weather there?” (cold, apparently)
  • “What does your father do?”
  • “When is the wedding?” (Melroy's — 26th, and yes, they'll ask again)
  • “Do you like India?” (a very enthusiastic yes)
  • “Can you eat spicy?” (say yes, then drink the lassi)

Small comfort

You are not on your own here

This is the most useful page on the site. If something goes wrong — lost, unwell, a scooter in a ditch, a family thing you don't understand — call me, or call anyone in that family tree. Nobody will be annoyed — that's what they're for.

And if you're too overwhelmed to talk to any of them? Go and sit on the verandah. Someone will bring you tea within four minutes and everything will feel manageable again.

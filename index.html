    Two things, quickly

    Before you get here

    Nobody is in a hurry

    “Coming in ten minutes” means twenty-five. Dinner at 8 means 8:45. This is not rudeness, it's a different relationship with clocks.

    Say yes to food

    Refusing once is polite. Refusing twice is a negotiation. Refusing three times is an insult. Take a small portion, eat it slowly, get adopted forever.

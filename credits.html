Everything else is drawn

The map, the house, the little beach strips, the sun on the icon — all original line drawings made for this site, in the hand-drawn Goan spirit of Mario Miranda. They are not his work, and no Miranda artwork is reproduced here.

If you own prints or licence his images, they'd sit beautifully in place of the drawings.

Type

Fonts

No webfonts are loaded — the site uses what's already on the device. On an iPhone that means Iowan Old Style for headings and Marker Felt for the handwritten labels, which is where a lot of the personality comes from.

On a device without Marker Felt it falls back to something similar, and the page still reads correctly.

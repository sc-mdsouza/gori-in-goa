    Everything, in order

    The same list as the calendar, laid out flat. This is the one to screenshot.

      Dress codes

      What to wear, where

      W

      Wedding: Indian formal if you want the full experience — a saree or lehenga can be borrowed and someone will tie it for you. Otherwise a smart dress. Avoid white and black at a Hindu wedding; avoid white at a Catholic one.

      C

      Church: shoulders and knees covered. Shoes off at the door if it's a temple.

      B

      Beach: anything. But you'll feel less conspicuous walking through the village in a cover-up.

      N

      Night out: a shawl. It's 26°C but the scooter ride home is not.

      Timing

      Indian Standard Time

      Everything on this calendar has a time. Treat it as a suggestion with good intentions.

      • Weddings: arrive 30–45 min after the stated time. You will still be early.
      • Dinner at home: this one is accurate. Be there.
      • Sunset: the only event in Goa that is never late. Arrive 20 min before.
      • Flights: leave three hours of buffer. Traffic is the one thing that is never delayed.
